Notes for the Coming Apocalypse

5.When the skies have become thickened
From industrial toxins and gases,
And the calming blue has sickened
Into leftover monochrome ashes,
Remember the trees that gave air.

4.And when our silent violence
Corrupts the harmony of Earth's music
And leaves a violent silence,
Remember birds singing with flair.

3.When rivers dry, and oceans rise,
When Mother Earth meets her demise,
Remember the water she'd share.

2.When the natural world is in despair,
Remember it's due to our lack of care.

1.Good luck.
